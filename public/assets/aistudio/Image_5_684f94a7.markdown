## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, data-driven environment tailored for numerical analytics, probability matrices, and statistical decision support surrounding Loto Bonheur Côte d'Ivoire. It rejects casino-like skeuomorphism, flashing neon tickers, and speculative hype. Instead, it positions itself as an academic-grade mathematical observatory: rigorous, sober, mathematically honest, and deeply grounded in precision.

The visual narrative bridges modern financial dashboard design with refined Ivorian dignity. Deep forest emerald conveys institutional longevity and grounding; warm golden amber functions strictly as an analytical marker for mathematical convergence, hot-zones, and probabilistic consensus. The aesthetic operates under a high-clarity data-dense framework: razor-sharp typography, disciplined low-contrast structural dividers, micro-scale tabular numbers, and dedicated visual encodings for lottery spheres (numbered 1 through 90) that feel tangible yet mathematically cataloged.

## Colors

The palette employs a controlled chromatic hierarchy designed to sustain prolonged technical review without cognitive fatigue:

- **Primary Forest Emerald (`#0F3E2E`)**: Represents institutional authority, stability, and primary navigation surfaces. An intensified deep tone (`#0A291E`) provides solid contrast for critical callouts and dark header bands.
- **Secondary Amber Gold (`#D97706`)**: Reserved exclusively for focal telemetry—frequent series, anomaly detections, and statistical consensus. Subdued amber (`#B45309`) serves as active hover and focused border states.
- **Tertiary Deep Slate (`#1E293B`)**: Delivers structural definition for text hierarchy, heavy table header cells, and chart axes.
- **Neutral Warm Slate Foundation (`#F8FAFC` to `#0F172A`)**: Base canvases use `#F8FAFC`, layered containers utilize clean optical white (`#FFFFFF`) with borders framed by `#E2E8F0`, and primary typography renders in `#0F172A` for absolute contrast.
- **Semantic & Ethical Tones**: Responsible play warnings and regulatory banners rely on an institutional sand-tinted gold (`#FEF3C7` container with `#92400E` border and typography) to distinguish advisory disclaimers from operational errors.

## Typography

Typography balances clean structural geometry via **Outfit** for structural headers with the analytical legibility of **Inter** across tabular displays, metrics, and narrative sections. 

All analytical numbers, frequency matrices, draw timestamps, and lottery indices must enable tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums; font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1, 'lnum' 1;`). This guarantees strict vertical alignment across dense multi-column arrays. `label-sm` utilizes an expanded tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`) in uppercase format for metric badges, standard deviations, and draw session headers (e.g., AKWABA, DIAMANT, BENEDICTION).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict vertical rhythms geared towards high data density:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with `margin` of `1rem` and `gutter` of `1rem`. Mathematical matrices collapse from tabular multi-column views into horizontally swipeable cards or compact stacked trace lists.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column structure with `margin` of `1.5rem` and `gutter` of `1rem`. Dual panel displays (filter left, matrix right).
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at an absolute max-width of `1600px` to maintain optimal data scanning geometry. Outer margins scale to `2.5rem` (`margin-desktop`) with `1.5rem` gutters (`gutter-desktop`).

Internal padding inside analytical containers uses compact increments (`space-sm` for table rows, `space-md` for standard card bodies) to maximize above-the-fold telemetry while preserving breathing room around primary statistical outcomes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy atmospheric drop shadows, aligning with the platform's analytical and scientific credibility:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Background rendered in warm slate `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px structural stroke of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow is microscopic: `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active States & Data Flyouts)**: Floating inspect tooltips and drop-down filters use `#FFFFFF` bounded by `#CBD5E1` with an ambient diffusion: `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 62, 46, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Deep Analysis Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 41, 30, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

Surfaces never simulate plastic or inflated glass bubbles; depth remains disciplined, structural, and engineered for clean visual ergonomics.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a restrained architectural corner curvature (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, base `0.25rem` / `4px`). Sharp precision conveys technical stability across metrics, tabular grids, input fields, and layout segmenters.

- Standard buttons, tabular headers, metric cards, and filter panels use `4px` corner radii.
- Dialog containers and primary statistical cards scale to `rounded-lg` (`8px`).
- The explicit exception to this rule is the **Lottery Ball Component (Balls 1 to 90)** and circular status tags, which are strictly circular (`rounded-full` / `50%`) to retain pure spherical iconography, creating an intentional contrast against the geometric dashboard environment.

## Components

### Lottery Number Badges (Balls 1 to 90)
- **Geometry**: Perfect circle (`w-9 h-9` standard table badge, `w-12 h-12` featured draw hero).
- **Default State**: Pure white background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`, typography `Outfit` Bold with tabular numbers in `#0F172A`.
- **Hot / High Frequency**: Soft amber fill `#FEF3C7`, 1.5px border `#D97706`, text `#B45309`.
- **Cold / Dormant**: Slate fill `#F1F5F9`, border `#94A3B8`, text `#64748B`.
- **Draw Highlight (Emerald)**: Solid `#0F3E2E` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner bevel highlight to convey physical presence without overt skeuomorphism.

### Buttons & Interactivity
- **Primary Action**: Deep emerald `#0F3E2E` solid background, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600. Hover transitions to `#0A291E`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` and border `#94A3B8`.
- **Amber Action (Consensus / Run Algorithm)**: Solid `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#B45309`.
- **Height Scale**: Compact data controls at `32px`; primary toolbar buttons at `40px`.

### Data Tables & Mathematical Traces
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase `11px` Inter font with `0.05em` tracking, 1px bottom border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Cell Padding**: `8px 12px` for high-density analysis. Hovering over a row highlights the entire track with `#F1F5F9`.
- **Mathematical Traces (Deviation, Variance, Gap)**: Monospaced numerical values. Positive delta renders in muted emerald `#047857`; negative or delayed intervals render in subdued slate-slate red `#B91C1C`.

### Tab Navigation
- Flush segmented bar style. Unselected tabs feature `#64748B` typography with no background. Active tab features an underline indicator in `#0F3E2E` (2px height) accompanied by medium font weight in `#0F3E2E`.

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes & Radio Buttons)
- **Input Fields**: Height `36px`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Focus state uses 1.5px `#0F3E2E` with an amber focus-ring glow of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square/circle with border `#94A3B8`. Selected fill `#0F3E2E` with white icon check/dot.

### Ethical & Legal Disclaimer Banner
- Permanent structural banner displayed at the root of algorithmic projections:
- **Surface**: Soft parchment cream `#FFFBEB`, bounded by 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
- **Left Indicator**: 4px vertical anchor bar in `#D97706`.
- **Content**: Explicit advisory stating that lottery draws are strictly independent stochastic events; platform tools provide descriptive retrospective analytics and probability modeling without outcome guarantees.
